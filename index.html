Stay within the walls

Soles is a multiplayer roleplay game set in Lock City, a walled city on a 12 km island. The government assigns your job. What you do after your shift is up to you.

  • 12 players per session
  • One persistent world
  • Proximity voice
  • Windows

Lock City

Gothic spires, palm-lined avenues and a ring of burnt-out blocks, all held inside one concrete wall with three gates. The sky over it is real: the clock, sun, moon and stars follow Augusta, Maine, right now.

Pay is in Scrap

You do not pick a career in Lock City. Go to a jobs board or open your phone, and the city government hands you one. Skill levels add 15% to your pay each.

Beyond the gates

Coming in is free. Leaving costs 50 Scrap. Past the toll, the island runs 12 km across, from harbours and desert to snow, with wolves and deer in the woods between.

Blackwater. Don’t go in.

Cypress, green fog and one boardwalk. Vampires hunt the swamp and grow stronger after dark. Eight cards are buried out here for anyone who digs at night and gets back.

Under your own flag

There are no preset gangs. Players found the factions, set the ranks, pick from 48 insignia, raise a base and defend it. The Lock City Guards answer with tasers, cuffs and a transport van.

  • Turf

    Three territories open at a time. Hold the flag and collect on every payday.

    90 s to capture · open 25 min

  • The government train

    A scrap shipment rolls every hour. Blow the vault, or escort it for the Guards.

    12,000 per bag · 1,500 escort

  • Cartel drop

    A ping goes out to the whole map, and everyone races for the crates.

    Drugs · marked cash · materials

  • Planned heists

    Plan from the board at home: a jewellery store hold-up or the Dry Creek casino cage.

    Crew of 1+ · crew of 2+

  • The Undercroft

    No gun shops here. Find The Broker in the sewers under the church, or craft your own.

    “Don’t ask mine.”

  • Bounties

    Every kill raises the price on your head.

    1,000 per kill · up to 25,000

Every wound has a name

Your body has 35 regions, and each wound bleeds on its own. Bandage it yourself, or get to a hospital for a splint or surgery. Go down and you have 90 seconds. Die and you drop your inventory and half your cash.

After the shift

  • Street racing

    Host from your phone. Downtown Loop, The Zigzag or Uptown Sprint, with buy-ins of 300 to 800.

  • Silver Spur

    Slots, roulette and blackjack, with bets from 10 to 5,000 Scrap.

  • Pool and Joker

    Eight-ball with real table physics, and a trick-taking card game that fills empty seats.

  • Pickup basketball

    One shared ball on the park court, and yes, you can steal it.

  • BMX

    Grab a bike from the rack at the skatepark for flips, barspins and tailwhips.

  • Graffiti

    Buy a can and paint freehand on a real canvas.

  • Farm and hunt

    Grow tomato, corn, potato and pumpkin, cook them at home, or track deer and wolves.

  • Radio

    Curfew FM, RXDXO FM and Frostback FM, from your car or home stereo.

  • Your own place

    Start at the motel. Cottages run 12,000 Scrap, and houses start at 45,000.

Thin pines and cliffs hide the best salvage, and the worst company.

Getting in

Continue with Discord, pick a Firstname_Lastname and wake up in the city with 2,500 Scrap in the bank. The first player in hosts the session. When the host leaves, anyone can reopen the same saved world.
